## About Me

I'm a PhD student at the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh, working under the mentorship of <a href="https://jingjieli.me/" target="_blank"> **Jingjie Li** </a> and <a href="https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/ppatras/" target="_blank"> **Paul Patras** </a>. Before that, I was a master student at State Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), working under the mentorship of Prof.<a href="http://hourui-arch.net/" target="_blank"> **Rui Hou** </a>. I obtained my Bachelor's degree with major in Software Engineering from the School of Software at Shandong University in 2022.

## Research Interests
My research focuses on systems security for autonomous LLM agent ecosystems, with an emphasis on securing communication and interaction protocols. My goal is to establish the security foundations for the emerging agentic stack. Specifically, I conduct systematic threat modeling of agent-tool and human-agent interfaces, empirically measure real-world deployment risks, and design system-level defenses that enforce strict trust boundaries and human control over agent actions and data flows. Previously, I worked on systems security for machine learning, focusing on practical and deployable defenses.
